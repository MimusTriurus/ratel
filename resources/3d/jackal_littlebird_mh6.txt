The download, unchanged, is resources/3d/ref/littlebird_mh6/little_bird_mh-6_low_poly.glb.

Changed from the original glb (the text block jackal_littlebird_mh6.py in the
blend does all of it, from the download):
- the Sketchfab/FBX empties removed and every part's transform applied; the
  whole scaled to 8.08 m nose to fin, the skids on the ground, the mast on
  the origin;
- the textures replaced by a flat colour for each part, olive drab, the
  canopy glass tinted blue and still see-through (alpha 0.45), the main blades' tips
  khaki and the tail rotor's orange; shaded smooth with the edges over 40
  degrees sharp;
- the parts joined into one mesh on a rig of three bones, Root and the two
  rotors, with a Fly clip turning them;
- a contour shell (a Solidify in an M6_Contour material) on the silhouette
  and the big parts, not on the canopy;
- added, not in the original: the MH-6's external personnel benches, a
  plank either side on the skids' struts, as an object of its own,
  UpBenches, on unless --no-heli-benches;
- added: the seats' marks, empties Seat_* (the benches' six, the cabin's two,
  the pilot's and the crewman's), where the preview sits people.
