low_poly_soldier.blend

"low poly soldier" by Kolos Studios (https://sketchfab.com/KolosStudios),

Changed from the original glb:
- the body's vertices scaled down 19.555 times to agree with the skin's
  inverse bind matrices (the M4's already did), which the FBX-to-glTF
  conversion had left apart, so that the model came apart when posed;
- 29 bones dropped: the FBX's "_end" leaf bones, which pointed at one stray
  point, and the unweighted IK helpers; the head's and the fingertips' bones
  given sensible lengths;
- the Sketchfab/FBX empties removed, the whole scaled to 1.8 m and applied,
  the rest (T) pose set on the ground;
- a Walk cycle added (text block soldier_walk.py in the blend, which makes
  it), beside the original Standing pose;
- the rifle and its magazine skinned to a bone of their own, `rifle`, rather
  than to the right hand (soldier_rifle.py), which follows the hand in
  Standing and Walk;
- Order (order arms, a breathing cycle) and Present (present arms, ending on
  the pose) added for the game over's honour guard (soldier_guard.py).
