low_poly_soldier.blend

"low poly soldier" by Kolos Studios (https://sketchfab.com/KolosStudios),

Changed from the original glb:
- the body's vertices scaled down 19.555 times to agree with the skin's
  inverse bind matrices (the M4's already did), which the FBX-to-glTF
  conversion had left apart, so that the model came apart when posed;
- 29 bones dropped: the FBX's "_end" leaf bones, which pointed at one stray
  point, and the unweighted IK helpers; the head's and the fingertips' bones
  given sensible lengths;
- the Sketchfab/FBX empties removed, the whole scaled to 1.8 m and applied,
  the rest (T) pose set on the ground;
- a Walk cycle added (text block soldier_walk.py in the blend, which makes
  it), beside the original Standing pose;
- the rifle and its magazine skinned to a bone of their own, `rifle`, rather
  than to the right hand (soldier_rifle.py), which follows the hand in
  Standing and Walk;
- Order (order arms, a breathing cycle) and Present (present arms, ending on
  the pose) added for the game over's two on post, and Attention, Salute
  (the hand to the helmet) and Walk_Unarmed (the arms swinging) for its
  guard, whose rifles the game hides (soldier_guard.py, soldier_walk.py);
- Aim (the rifle in the shoulder, a breathing cycle), Shoot (one round's
  kick) and Death (thrown back, down onto his back, the rifle dropped beside
  him) added for the stage's enemy (soldier_fire.py), and Wave (the right
  arm up over the head, waving) and Run_Unarmed (a jog, the arms bent) for
  its prisoners and the rescue helicopter's crewman (soldier_friend.py,
  soldier_walk.py);
- Sit (a passenger), Sit_Hold (on a bench, holding its edge) and Sit_Pilot
  (the hands on the cyclic and the collective) added for the rescue
  helicopter's prisoners, crewman and pilot (soldier_sit.py), each standing
  on its seat's point rather than on the ground;
- Talk (standing easy, the right hand making his points, palm up) and
  Listen (the arms hanging, nodding) added for the shop's pilot and jeep's
  man going over the supply by turns (soldier_talk.py);
- a contour shell (a Solidify in a Soldier_Contour material) on every mesh,
  and the glb, low_poly_soldier.glb, exported with those clips and Walk
  (soldier_export.py).
